How It Started

Born from the belief that gentle is powerful.

Maekkeuleoun started with a simple frustration: why do most soaps leave your skin feeling stripped, tight, and dull after every wash? We knew there had to be a better way — one that respects your skin's natural balance while still delivering a truly clean feeling.

We went back to basics. We studied clean, natural ingredients. We tested gently. We refined carefully. And we created two products — Bel Soap for her and Bo Soap for him — that deliver real results without the harsh chemistry.

Today, Maekkeuleoun is more than a skincare brand. It's a promise: that your skin will always be treated with the honesty and care it deserves. Because authentic glow starts with authentic ingredients.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every bar we make is guided by these three principles.

🌿

Clean Ingredients

We source genuine, skin-loving ingredients — no harsh sulfates, no unnecessary chemicals. What goes on your skin should be something you can trust completely.

💧

Gentle Effectiveness

Gentle doesn't mean weak. Our formulas are crafted to cleanse deeply, hydrate thoroughly, and protect your skin barrier — all without stripping or irritating.

✨

Authentic Glow

Real glow comes from healthy, nourished skin — not from harsh treatments or temporary fixes. We're here for your long-term skin health and confidence.

For Everyone
